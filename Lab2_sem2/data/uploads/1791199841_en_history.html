A Brief History of Computing

The first electronic general-purpose computer was ENIAC, completed in 1945. It used vacuum tubes and occupied a large room. The invention of the transistor in 1947 and later the integrated circuit enabled much smaller and more reliable machines. The microprocessor, introduced in the early 1970s, brought computing power to individuals and led to the personal computer revolution.

The development of the Internet and the World Wide Web in the 1990s transformed how people communicate and access information. Mobile devices and cloud computing have further changed the landscape in the twenty-first century. Artificial intelligence is now becoming a central technology in many industries.
